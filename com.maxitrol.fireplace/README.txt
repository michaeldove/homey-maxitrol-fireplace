Make your gas fireplace part of everyday Homey routines with convenient on/off control, flame adjustment and Flow actions. Pairing learns the unique identity of your existing Maxitrol G6R H4T 433.92 MHz handset, letting Homey communicate with the receiver while retaining the original fireplace controls and safety systems.

Requires a Homey installation with access to 433 MHz radio hardware. Homey Self-Hosted Server installations require a Homey Bridge for RF transmission. This community integration has been physically verified with the G6R-H4T5-Z19 handset and G6R-R4AU receiver. Other G6R H4T variants may use a compatible protocol, but B6R/Symax, Wi-Fi, 315 MHz and 868 MHz systems are not supported. Communication is transmit-only, so Homey cannot detect changes made with the original handset and displays the last command sent through Homey. Always operate the fireplace responsibly and do not automate ignition while it is unattended.
